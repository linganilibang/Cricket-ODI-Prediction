        What do these numbers mean?
        Average
        Runs scored for each time the batter was out.
        Strike rate
        Runs scored per 100 balls faced.
        Bats at
        The batting position they usually take. 1 and 2 are the openers.
        Boundary %
        The share of their runs that came from fours and sixes.
        Economy
        Runs given away per over bowled. Lower is better.
        Average (bowlers)
        Runs given away for each wicket taken. Lower is better.
        Balls per wicket
        How often the bowler takes a wicket. Lower is better.
        Dot-ball %
        The share of balls bowled that went for no runs.
